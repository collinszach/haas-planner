# Haas Planner — Product Context

Haas Planner is the unified 4-semester course planning, degree-audit, and automatic Core Swap platform for UC Berkeley Haas Full-Time MBA students.

## Core User Jobs
1. **Plan 4 Semesters (`Y1 Fall`, `Y1 Spring`, `Y2 Fall`, `Y2 Spring`)**: Combine Cohort Core courses (Fall A/B & Spring A/B), Haas MBA electives, and UC Berkeley graduate/upper-division non-MBA courses (`classes.berkeley.edu` via Berkeleytime) in one place.
2. **Block Time in 30-Minute Increments (`08:00–21:30`)**: Click or drag directly across 30-minute intervals on the weekly calendar (Part A / Part B) to reserve recruiting interviews, GSI teaching, family commitments, or commute blocks.
3. **Discover Classes That Fit ("Fits My Schedule")**: Surface courses that have zero overlap with Core sections, planned electives, and personal 30-minute blocks — as well as near-fit classes unlocked by a Core Swap — displaying Professor, Course Worth (`/7`), Instructor Rating (`/7`), Ease / Level of Work (`Difficulty /7`), Historical Bid-Out Risk, and exact Degree Audit Impact ("What it fills").
4. **Auto-Generated Core Swaps**: Picking electives or blocking 30-minute windows that intersect a Spring 2027 Core section automatically computes and stages the required Single, Paired (All-or-Nothing), or Cohort swap with live FIFO queue liquidity and 1-click submission. Confirming a matched swap updates the student's schedule across both the swap board and the Y1 Spring semester plan.
5. **Track Haas Graduation Requirements**: Live audit against FTMBA rules (11 Core courses / 20 units, 51 valid MBA units, 1 Experiential Learning course, Business & Tech flags, max 5 non-Haas units, and optional Certificate progress across BA, SB, AI, RE).

## Operational Constraints
- `@berkeley.edu` OAuth authentication for live student accounts, plus full Demo Sandbox parity.
- FIFO chronological matching queue (`Single`, `Paired All-or-Nothing`, `Cohort`), anonymized live queue & Market Pulse, and Coordinator lifecycle resets (`End Swap Cycle`, `Reset Academic Year & Cohorts`, `Edit Core Time Slots`).
